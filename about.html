03 — About

Cooking came to me through self-teaching, and photography through a long practice of learning to really see the world around me. Over time, the two stopped feeling separate. Both come down to the same things: patience, passion, and a creative eye for detail.

Right now I'm in culinary school, working toward a professional skillset while continuing to build my photography under Soul & Horizon. Alongside that, I bring a background in administrative and operational work, the kind of behind-the-scenes support that keeps creative projects moving.

I'm based in Sonoma County and the greater Bay Area, and the place shapes everything I make. Seasonal food, local sourcing, the landscapes and wildlife I live among. What draws me most is where cooking and image meet, and how food photography and styling can bring those two practices into the same frame.

"Both disciplines ask for the same thing: presence. You can't rush a reduction any more than you can rush the light."
