About

Cooking came to me through self-teaching, and photography through a long practice of learning to really see the world around me. Spending time noticing light, texture, and small shifts in a scene changed the way I approached both. Over time, the two stopped feeling separate. They began to overlap in quiet ways, in how I plate, how I notice color, and how I think about composition. Both come down to patience, attention, and a creative eye for detail.

Most of my photography happens on slow outings close to home: marshes, coastline, back roads, and the wildlife that lives along them. I shoot under the name Soul & Horizon, and this site is where that work lives, along with notes from those outings and the food I’m cooking in between.

I’m based in Sonoma County, and the place shapes everything I make. Seasonal food, local sourcing, and the landscapes and wildlife I live among all influence both what I cook and how I see.
