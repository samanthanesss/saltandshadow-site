About

I come from self-taught cooking and a long practice of learning to see. Over time, the two stopped feeling separate—both rooted in patience, attention, and detail.

I’m currently in culinary school, building toward a professional skillset, while continuing to develop my photography work under Soul & Horizon. I also bring experience in administrative and operational work that supports creative projects behind the scenes.

Based in Sonoma County and the greater Bay Area, my work is shaped by a connection to place—seasonal food, local sourcing, and the landscapes and wildlife that surround it. I’m interested in the intersection of cooking and image, and in using food photography and styling to bring those practices together.
