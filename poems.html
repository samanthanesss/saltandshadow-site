Poems

Some photos ask for words. These are the poems that came from them. More at @letterstothequietsky.

Sentinel

Black against the clean blue, you stand where nothing hides. Stone beneath your feet, sky behind your eyes, waiting without urgency.

You do not shout. You do not leave. You watch.

And that is the warning.

Still Speaking

Layers argue without sound. Paint over paint, names crossed out, truths half-buried and still visible.

Nothing here is polished. Everything here insists.

What was erased refuses to disappear. What was shouted once learned how to whisper and last.

This is how resistance survives not clean, not singular, but patient.

A wall that remembers every hand that said I was here and would not be undone.
